# Who are you?


Hey! I’m Scott, from Scotland, and the best name I could come up with for this project is `ScotSec`!

I’m a 30-something who worked as an electrical engineer for over 10 years and as an operations technician for 6. This website was created to host my blog posts and thoughts from my journey towards becoming a penetration tester and working as one.

# What is your experience?

Since starting this blog I ended up becoming a pentester and even senior pentester, now I hold certifications such as OSCP, OSEP, CPTS, CAPE and CRTO.

Previously have worked in various industries as an electrical engineer, including FMCG, brewing, and packaging. This involved everything from general planned and breakdown maintenance to programming and fault-finding on PLCs, as well as project work.

Though I was a long-time hobbyist, I started my journey with no formal IT or cybersecurity experience in January 2024. I hadn’t even used Linux before I started, so there was quite a learning curve. Hopefully, I can help show others that it is possible to start a career in penetration testing from scratch if you are willing to put in the time and effort while utilizing skills from previous roles.
